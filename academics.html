Our Academic Programs

Nurturing intelligence and creativity through a comprehensive curriculum.

The UNEB Curriculum

We offer both 'O' and 'A' Level programs with a wide range of subjects.

O-Level (UCE)

A four-year program focusing on foundational knowledge and critical thinking under the new lower secondary curriculum.

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Geography
  • History
  • Agriculture
  • Commerce
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Fine Art
  • Computer Studies
  • Physical Education
  • Kiswahili
  • Religious Education (C.R.E)

A-Level (UACE)

A two-year specialized program preparing students for university education through various Science and Arts combinations.

  • Science Combos: BCM, PEM, PCB, MEG, BCM/Sub
  • Arts Combos: HEG, HEL, DEG, HEG/Div, HED
  • General Paper (G.P)
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • Geography
  • History
  • Economics
  • Divinity / C.R.E
  • Literature in English
  • Fine Art
  • Sub-Math / ICT

Our Academic Philosophy

At Kennedy Secondary School, we don't just teach for exams; we teach for life. Our academic approach is centered on:

  • Practical Learning: Well-equipped laboratories for Science and ICT.
  • Continuous Assessment: Regular testing and feedback to track progress.
  • Mentorship: Dedicated teachers who guide students both academically and morally.
