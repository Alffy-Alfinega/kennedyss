Application Process

We've streamlined our admission process to make it as smooth as possible for both students and parents. Follow these simple steps to join the KSS family.

1

Initial Inquiry

Visit the school campus in Kawuku or fill out the online inquiry form on this page to express interest.

2

Form Collection & Submission

Pick up an admission form from the school office (UGX 50,000) and submit it with all required documents.

3

Interviews & Placement

Prospective students will undergo a brief interview and placement assessment to determine their academic needs.

4

Admission Letter

Successful applicants will receive an official admission letter along with the full school requirements list.
