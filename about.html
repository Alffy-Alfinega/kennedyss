About Our School

A legacy of excellence, faith, and transformation.

Our Story

Kennedy Secondary School (KSS) is a premier private secondary school located 22km along the Kampala-Entebbe Highway in Kawuku. Since its inception, KSS has been dedicated to providing a supportive and inspirational environment for young enquiring minds to learn and grow.

As a Seventh-day Adventist (SDA) affiliated institution, we believe in the holistic development of our students—nurturing their academic, spiritual, physical, and social dimensions. Our campus, often called the "Kenhill Campus," is designed to be a safe haven for learning and character building.

We are particularly renowned for our exceptional music program, which has produced world-class choirs that perform both locally and internationally, carrying our message of faith and excellence through song.

Our Vision

To be the best centre of holistic learning at secondary education with a global impact, producing God-fearing and productive citizens.

Our Mission

To provide quality, holistic education that transforms students into productive and engaged members of society through a Christ-centered environment.

SDA Core Values

  • Faith: Trust in God as the source of all wisdom.
  • Integrity: Honesty and transparency in all actions.
  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in academics and character.
  • Service: Commitment to helping others and the community.
  • Stewardship: Responsible care of God-given talents and resources.

Our Global Alumni Network

KSS graduates are making an impact in various fields across the globe.

Continuing the Legacy

Our alumni are our greatest ambassadors. From medical doctors in the UK to engineers in the USA and entrepreneurs within East Africa, the foundation laid at KSS continues to yield fruits.

We maintain a strong connection with our former students through the KSS Alumni Association, which provides mentorship to current students and supports school development projects.

Our Journey

A look at the milestones that have shaped Kennedy Secondary School.

1998

Foundation of Kennedy Primary School, laying the groundwork for excellence in Kawuku.

2005

Inception of Kennedy Secondary School (KSS) to provide high-quality secondary education.

2012

Establishment of the world-renowned "Stream of Life Choir", bringing national fame to KSS.

2020

Modernization of the "Kenhill Campus" with state-of-the-art Science and ICT laboratories.
